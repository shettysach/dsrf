# Task: box push

## Objective

Use both hands to hold the movable box and push it straight forward until it is
centered on the green goal. Then stand.

## Procedure

1. Face the box squarely and approach it from behind.
2. Stop close to the box while remaining centered behind it.
3. Reach both hands forward to the near face of the box. Put one hand on each
   side of its center and establish firm hand contact before moving it.
4. Keep both hands in contact with the box and push them **straight forward**.
   Walk forward only as needed to maintain the hand hold.
5. Continue the forward hand push until the box is fully inside and centered on
   the green goal.
6. Stop pushing immediately, release the box, and issue **stand**.

## Alignment and safety

- Keep the robot, box, and green goal on one straight centerline before making
  hand contact.
- Push through the center of the box with the hands; do not bump, kick, or push
  it with the torso, legs, or feet.
- Do not push from a corner or move sideways while holding the box. If contact
  or alignment is lost, step back, realign behind the box, re-establish a
  two-hand hold, and resume the forward push.
- The green goal is a visual marker only; the box is the movable object.
