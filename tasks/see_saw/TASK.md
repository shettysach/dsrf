# Task: see-saw

## Objective

Walk onto the lateral hinged see-saw and stand where your weight balances the
red counterweight. Success means remaining upright while the blue plank is
approximately level and no longer swinging.

## Scene

- The blue plank spans left-to-right and pivots around the gray support beneath
  its center.
- A red counterweight is fixed to one lateral end.
- The plank's long edges are directly to the robot's left and right.

## Procedure

1. Approach the center of the plank's near edge in a straight line.
2. Step forward onto the plank near its central hinge line.
3. Move sideways toward the end opposite the red counterweight using short
   movements.
4. Watch the plank angle after every movement and allow oscillation to settle.
5. If your side drops too far, move slightly back toward the center. If the
   counterweight side remains low, move slightly farther from the center.

## Safety

- Stay on the plank's longitudinal centerline.
- Do not walk into or climb onto the red counterweight.
- Use sideways commands for balancing and forward/backward commands only to
  remain centered across the plank's narrow dimension.
- Do not chase each swing with rapid alternating commands; wait and reassess.

## Finish

Stand when the plank is close to level, motion has settled, and the robot is
upright with both feet securely on the blue surface.
