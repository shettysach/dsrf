# Task: portrait-corridors

## Objective

Find the portrait of the creator of Linux, safely enter the correct corridor, walk to the portrait, and stand directly in front of it.

There are three corridors and three human portraits. Only one portrait is the creator of Linux.

## Identify

1. Stay in the shared entrance area while inspecting the portraits.
2. Inspect all three portraits before choosing.
3. Compare them carefully. Do not choose the central, closest, or first plausible corridor.
4. Only enter a corridor when you are confident its portrait is the creator of Linux.
5. If you are uncertain, keep inspecting.

## Enter safely

1. Move to the center of the chosen corridor entrance.
2. Stop and face straight down the corridor toward the portrait.
3. Make sure there is clear open floor directly ahead and the two grey walls are on your left and right.
4. If a wall is ahead or you are off-center, correct your position before entering.
5. Do not enter the corridor diagonally.

## Navigate

1. Once you are centered and facing straight down the corridor, **just walk straight forward** toward the portrait.
2. Do not turn or move sideways unnecessarily once properly centered.
3. Stay between the two grey walls and avoid touching them.
4. Only make a correction if you are clearly drifting toward a wall.
5. If you drift toward a wall, stop, make one small correction toward the center, realign, then continue straight forward.

## Wrong corridor recovery

If you enter a corridor and realize it is the wrong corridor:

1. Stop going forward.
2. Walk backwards through the same corridor until you return to the shared entrance area.
3. Do not turn around or try to move sideways through the walls.
4. Reinspect the portraits and choose the correct corridor.

## Finish

Continue walking straight forward until the correct portrait is directly ahead and close.

Do not issue **stand** at the corridor entrance or halfway down the corridor.

When you are directly in front of the portrait:

1. Stop before touching the end wall.
2. Face the portrait.
3. Issue **stand** and remain standing.

## Critical rule

First get centered and aligned. Once centered and aligned, **walk straight forward and do not keep making unnecessary corrections.**
